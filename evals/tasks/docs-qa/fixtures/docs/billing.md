# Billing

Monthly plans are invoiced on the first day of each month for the previous
month's usage. Annual plans are invoiced on the contract anniversary date.

Payment terms are **net 30**: invoices must be paid within 30 days of the
invoice date. Overdue balances accrue a late fee of 1.5% per month. After 45
days overdue, API keys are restricted to read-only access until payment.

Invoices are sent to the billing contact and are also available in the console
under Settings → Billing. Purchase order numbers can be added to future invoices.
